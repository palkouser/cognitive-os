## What `not_opened` means here

Fifteen conditions are marked `not_opened` rather than failed or skipped, and the distinction is
the whole point of the state. Nothing about them was measured and found wanting: their evidence
would have been produced by work that a pre-registered stop closed before it could open. Each row
binds the same stop hash, so a reader can check that fifteen rows were closed by one decision
rather than by fifteen judgement calls.

Condition 20 is the one worth reading in full. Its precondition *was* measured, and the
measurement is the stop: over 100 independent fresh certification decisions the split-conformal
bar at α = 0.20 admits 0.40 of them with 6 errors, and the Clopper–Pearson 95% upper bound on the
error rate among those admitted is 0.2747 against the pre-registered ceiling of 0.15.

Condition 22 is the second. `CorrectionArtifactPayloadV3` is released and its loader was exercised
on a fixture in S21D6-024, and the sprint still cannot claim it: no candidate names a conformal
bar to bind into an artifact, and a record about a fixture is not a record about a candidate. D4
declined to blur that distinction, D5 declined to blur it in the other direction, and D6 declines
to blur it with a bar that exists but was not selected.
