certification decisions the seven-channel `containment-contrastive-linear-v1` direction admits
0.59 at α = 0.20 with a Clopper–Pearson 95% upper bound of **0.126207** against a ceiling of
0.15, and scores **0.9492** first choice over what it admits against a 0.61 baseline. On 60 final
groups opened once after five sprints carried, it scores **0.8667** against **0.4833** — a gain of
**+0.3833** with a paired group bootstrap of **[0.233, 0.533]** excluding zero.

And the sentence that belongs beside every one of those numbers: **under the seated containment
rung, only 5 of 59 admitted decisions differ from the ordering the containment channel produces
on its own.** 5.085 projected changed final decisions against a floor of 20. The pass exists
because S21D7-027 unseated that rung at W2 step 0, before anything was scored, on the gate
owner's explicit decision with both branches priced. The honest reading of this gate is that a
deterministic, label-free, pre-sandbox ordering is most of the value and the fitted direction is
the remainder.

stops the moment it is told to.

and is the paragraph above.

was never entered, and no surface outside the five routed canary groups consults it.
